I'm Sam — a Biomedical Science graduate from the University of Sheffield with a particular interest in the molecular underpinnings of cancer. My undergraduate dissertation explored mathematical modelling of ceRNA (competing endogenous RNA) networks, combining systems biology, dynamical modelling, and RNA biology to understand how non-coding RNAs regulate gene expression in disease.

This autumn I begin my MSc in Cancer at University College London, where I'll deepen my expertise in cancer biology and develop the research skills to tackle open questions in the field. My long-term goal is to pursue a PhD and contribute to research that translates into better outcomes for patients.

I'm particularly drawn to the intersection of computational and experimental approaches — whether that's building models of RNA regulatory networks or using bioinformatics to find patterns in genomic data.
